# Profile asset credits

`trophies.svg` is generated by [GitHub Profile Trophy](https://github.com/ryo-ma/github-profile-trophy), served by the community endpoint `https://trophy.ryglcloud.net/` listed in its upstream README. The trophy artwork/code is used under its [MIT license](licenses/trophies.txt), with the original copyright notice preserved. The profile embeds a checked-in SVG so an unavailable rendering service does not leave a broken image.
